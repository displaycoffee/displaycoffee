## Hello world! 👋

I'll try not to make this too boring. Does anyone even look at these things? Anyway…

It all started ages ago on a dark and stormy night ⛈️, a full moon 🌕 hanging in the midnight sky. A wizard came to my mom at the hospital 🏥 and said, "This child... she must create the pixels... put them in order and then there will be harmony and balance in the world!" 🧙‍♂️ After, this bearded guy was arrested 👮and I'm not sure what happened, but years later, the wizard was not entirely incorrect. I've been coding for as long as I can remember (fifteen years or more) and specialize in Frontend Development. Pixel conjuring was learned on my own and I didn't attend a traditional coding school; rather I learned by using the ancient technology of WebTV, Geocities, and Angelfire 🔥. In the time I've spent perfecting my craft, I will admit to certain atrocities (animated backgrounds and table layouts), but I’ve also advanced and spread this knowledge to others, helping them learn the mystical art of pixel rendering.

I'm not sure I've got the pixels in order yet, but I love what I do. It is my hope that this passion will bring a little light (and magic) to the world. 🌎
